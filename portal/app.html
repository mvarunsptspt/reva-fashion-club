What’s new

Portal update 16.0 — here is what changed:

  • Events & calendar — create events, RSVP, and see everything in one shared club calendar.
  • Fees & budget — membership dues, pending list, and an income-vs-expenses budget tracker.
  • Polls & voting — vote on show themes, costumes and songs.
  • Tasks — officials can assign tasks with deadlines; see yours under My tasks.
  • Club gallery — event and rehearsal photos, uploaded by officials.
  • Notification bell — alerts for announcements, tasks, leave decisions and events.
  • Theme toggle — switch the portal between dark and light from the header; dark stays the default.
  • Global search — one box in the header to find members, announcements, events and files.
  • Font size & reduce motion — new options in Settings.
